Chloris verticillata — Master Plant Pool row 259, reviewed 2026-10-10. Learner source-pool approval distinct from county-native certification.
Accepted POWO identity: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:991601-1
Existing approved Master Plant Pool diagnostic review: Look first for widely separated panicle whorls with long racemes (~5–15 cm), often with one terminal upright branch. Then dissect a mature spikelet: measure the fertile lemma awn (~4.8–9 mm) and sterile lemma awn (~3.2–7 mm) separately.
Look-alikes: C. cucullata has crowded 2–5 cm racemes, much shorter awns and an inflated rounded sterile hood. C. virgata is usually annual with racemes mainly in one terminal whorl and long apical marginal hairs on fertile lemma. Hybrid C. × subdolichostachya is intermediate; detached heads alone are not diagnostic.
Target zone UNRESOLVED, not confirmed target native. C. × subdolichostachya is an accepted hybrid of C. cucullata × C. verticillata; preserve as distinct reference, do not collapse into parents.

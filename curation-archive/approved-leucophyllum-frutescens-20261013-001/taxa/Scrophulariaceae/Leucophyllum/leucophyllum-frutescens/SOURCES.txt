LEUCOPHYLLUM FRUTESCENS — approved source-pool taxon, Master Plant Pool row 636.
Source flags: My iNaturalist Flora + personal list; nine total observations (six wild-tagged, three cultivated); "Hays Local" observation flag is NOT three-county native proof.
Taxonomic identity: Leucophyllum frutescens (Berland.) I.M.Johnst.; family Scrophulariaceae, NOT Salvia / Lamiaceae.

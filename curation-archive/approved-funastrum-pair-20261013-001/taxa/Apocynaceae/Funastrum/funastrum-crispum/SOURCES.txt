DIAGNOSIS: often crisped/undulate triangular leaves, large (~20mm) orange-brown-green rotate flowers in umbel-like cyme, five constricted flesh corona lobes; lower internodes short recurved hairs. Opposite heart-based leaves; do not separate on wavy edge alone (variable). Compare F. cynanchoides smaller cream/rose flower with unconstricted swollen white corona lobes, flatter leaf margins.
EXCLUSION: Funastrum heterophyllum is not automatically an F. crispum synonym in 2026 Kew accepted concepts. Needs range-level review if user brings trans-Pecos records.

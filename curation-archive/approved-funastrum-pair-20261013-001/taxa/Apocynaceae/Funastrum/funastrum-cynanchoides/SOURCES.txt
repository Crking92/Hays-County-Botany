DIAGNOSIS: opposite cordate ovate-to-lanceolate mostly flat-edged leaves; small cream/rose fringed petals 3–6mm; five swollen glossy white corona lobes, no median constriction. Compare F. crispum larger brown/orange-green corollas, crisped leaves, constricted corona. Talayote (Cynanchum unifarium) many tiny green-white flowers on racemelike branching axillary cluster, differing corona/pod.
POLISH: local leaf and flower same-scale pair, full vine habit, pod and silky seeds, glandular hair/central corona macro; labels/arrows after image-specific verification.

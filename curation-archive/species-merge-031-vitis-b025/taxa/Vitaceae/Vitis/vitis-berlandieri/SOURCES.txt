2. Kerr County — the Kerr Wildlife Management Area vascular flora vouchers Vitis cinerea var. helleri; the Heller type material is also from Kerrville.
3. Bexar County — Berlandier type/isotype specimens of Vitis berlandieri were collected at/near San Antonio and the San Antonio River.

LOOK HERE FIRST
Start with the lower leaf surface and fruit bloom. Berlandier/Heller's grape has a lower leaf surface that remains visible—sparsely webby/hairy to nearly glabrous—not a solid white felt. Branchlets are slightly angled and usually lack red-banded nodes; ripe berries are conspicuously glaucous and the fruiting clusters are comparatively short and compact.

Taxon: Oxalis dillenii Jacq.
Source-pool: Master Plant Pool row 801; learner-approved, not a confirmed three-county native.
Taxonomy / morphology: Flora of North America, https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=250101490
Taxonomy / range: Plants of the World Online, Kew; see Master Plant Pool taxonomy review for accepted concept and Texas caveat.
Range status: UNRESOLVED; Accepted FNA/POWO concept, recorded in Texas; project three-target-county native threshold not independently verified. Source-pool approval does not certify county nativity.
Morphological distinction: O. stricta often has spreading septate hairs especially near nodes, usually more flowers (often 5–7), and shorter 8–15 mm capsules; O. albicans has short curved/crisped nonseptate hairs rather than the straight closely appressed hairs of O. dillenii. O. corniculata often roots at creeping nodes and shows conspicuous broad free stipule flanges.

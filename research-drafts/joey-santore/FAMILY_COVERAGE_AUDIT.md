This is a coverage audit, not a claim that Joey is the canonical authority for taxonomy or formal range. Joey remains a high-weight source for field morphology, diagnostic characters, ecology and visual teaching; accepted names, family definitions, formal range and exceptional characters are reconciled against authoritative botanical sources.

## Highest-priority gaps

1. **Poaceae** — not a blank slate, but exact macro coverage of ligule/collar/sheath/spikelets is still the single biggest morphology need because the family contains 42 local species and 10 priority-1 species.
2. **Ranunculaceae** — build a verified Clematis drummondii/texensis package and a broader family comparison.
3. **Cannabaceae vs Ulmaceae** — locally important woody ID problem; Celtis and Ulmus need a deliberate side-by-side module.
4. **Convolvulaceae** — high local relevance and essentially no Joey family coverage; build around Ipomoea.
5. **Passifloraceae + Vitaceae** — create a vine-comparison module with tendril position and reproductive structures.
6. **Bignoniaceae / Plantaginaceae / Acanthaceae** — bilateral tubular-flower families need a comparison lesson so flower shape alone does not drive IDs.
7. **Apiaceae** — umbels, leaf sheaths and schizocarps need a clean family module.
8. **Papaveraceae** — Argemone is locally useful and visually distinctive; this should be easy to backfill well.
9. **Juglandaceae / Fagaceae** — Joey makes Fagaceae strong, but Juglandaceae needs enough material for an explicit nut/catkin/leaf comparison.
10. **Sapotaceae / Ebenaceae / Aquifoliaceae** — build a small woody-fruit comparison around Sideroxylon, Diospyros and Ilex.

## Build rule

For each family page, the learner should answer in this order:

1. **Where do I look first?**
2. **What 2–4 structural characters matter most?**
3. **What local family am I most likely to confuse it with?**
4. **What single structure breaks that tie?**
5. **Which exact images show those structures?**
6. **Which local genera/species should I practice on next?**

Do not let color, general habit or a single taxon become the family definition.

LOOK HERE FIRST
Look at the leaf axil. Drummond's ruellia has purple flowers/cymes essentially sessile in the axils rather than raised on a terminal stalk. The ovate leaves and stems have short fine pubescence, not the conspicuous long hairs of R. humilis. If fruit is present, the capsule's only 2–4 seeds are a powerful confirmation.

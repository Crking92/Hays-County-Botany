LOOK HERE FIRST
Flip the leaf first. Mustang grape has a dense white-to-rusty woolly tomentum on the lower surface that essentially hides the blade surface and veins. Then confirm broad heart- to kidney-shaped leaves and large dark berries usually 12 mm or more.

## Taxonomy
Accepted: Rhus aromatica Aiton
Kew/POWO: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A30016232-2
Current accepted infraspecifics include var. aromatica, var. pilosissima, var. simplicifolia.
Kew synonymizes Rhus trilobata and R. aromatica var./subsp. flabelliformis under R. aromatica var. aromatica.
NPSOT: https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/rhus-

## Morphology
Williamson County treatment and NPSOT: low deciduous suckering shrub; exactly three aromatic leaflets, often lobed/coarsely toothed; early compact yellowish flowers; red drupes.

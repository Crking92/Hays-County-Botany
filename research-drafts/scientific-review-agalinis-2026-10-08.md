# Agalinis diagnostic review — 2026-10-08

Scientific-review handoff only. No canonical installation, READY markers, native-range PASS, or gallery changes.

## Taxonomy
Kew POWO accepts Agalinis edwardsiana Pennell and Agalinis heterophylla (Nutt.) Small ex Britton in Orobanchaceae. Gerardia edwardsiana and A. edwardsiana var. glabra map to A. edwardsiana. Gerardia heterophylla and Aureolaria heterophylla map to A. heterophylla. Do not create duplicate species cards for synonyms.

## LOOK HERE FIRST
Examine and measure the complete pedicel between flower calyx and stem attachment. A. edwardsiana has conspicuously longer pedicels (to approximately 30 mm in Flora of North Central Texas); A. heterophylla has very short pedicels (approximately 1.6–3.2 mm in regional accounts). Confirm A. edwardsiana with very short calyx lobes and limestone-site context; confirm A. heterophylla with conspicuous calyx lobes and sometimes three-cleft lower leaves. Habitat is supporting evidence, not proof.

Do NOT split these species by pink/purple flower color, narrow leaves, or right-angle branching. UT Austin PlantPics explicitly reports right-angle branches in both. The three-cleft lower leaves of A. heterophylla are optional, not universal. Reported pedicel maxima are not absolute diagnostic thresholds; assess multiple flowers and calyx structure.

## Range / status
Both names are accepted and Texas-native at state scale per Kew. Strict three-target-county certification is NOT inferred. Flora of North Central Texas reports A. edwardsiana in Burnet and Travis; Comal image records provide a verification lead. Preserve source-pool learner approval and existing separate range state.

# Deep Review sources — Clematis reticulata
Reviewed: 2026-10-02
Batch: deep-review-batch-010-clematis-b006
Status: TAXONOMY/RANGE FAIL FOR PROJECT SOURCE-POOL CONCEPT

Current taxonomy
Kew/POWO C. reticulata s.s.: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A60413-2
Kew's current distribution lists Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Mississippi, and South Carolina — not Texas.

Legacy morphology
FNA historical broad concept: https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=233500410
This older treatment includes Texas and is useful for understanding why older Texas lists still contain the name.

2022 revision
Murphy, T.H., Harris, J., & Estes, L.D. 2022. Morphometric and Molecular Evidence Delimit Six Species in Clematis reticulata s.l. Systematic Botany 47(3):667–690.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1600/036364422X16573019348210
Dryad dataset/abstract: https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.d2547d832

The revision recognizes multiple segregates, including Clematis arenicola for the West Gulf Coastal Plain lineage extending into eastern Texas. Current Kew recognizes C. arenicola as an accepted species. The project's Hays-local C. reticulata flag is not transferred to C. arenicola because Hays is outside that modern East-Texas/West-Gulf pattern and the source record needs re-identification.

Legacy NPSOT page (use with taxonomy warning): https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/clematis-reticulata/

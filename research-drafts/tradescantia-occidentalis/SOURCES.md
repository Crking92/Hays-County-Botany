LOOK HERE FIRST
Start with the narrow leaves and the stem hairs. Prairie spiderwort has narrow linear to strap-shaped leaves and conspicuous long-stalked glandular hairs on the internodes and peduncles. In the Williamson treatment the blades are only about 6–17 mm wide, which gives the plant a noticeably grassier look than giant spiderwort.

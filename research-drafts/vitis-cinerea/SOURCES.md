VITIS CINEREA — DEEP REVIEW SOURCES

Status: RANGE HOLD

Taxonomy
Accepted name: Vitis cinerea (Engelm.) Millardet
https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A1038332-2

Range evidence
1. Kew/POWO accepts Vitis cinerea and recognizes Texas in the native range, but does not include var. helleri among its accepted infraspecifics.
2. Flora of North America states var. cinerea is found in Texas only in the eastern part of the state and intergrades with var. helleri southwest of the Brazos River.
3. USDA-derived species-level county data include Central Texas counties, but those records may aggregate the older helleri/berlandieri concept and are therefore not used to satisfy the three-county rule.
Decision: UNRESOLVED — fewer than three exact target-zone counties verified under the current concept.

LOOK HERE FIRST
Treat this as a concept-check species. In the strict var. cinerea morphology, branchlets are distinctly angled and densely short-hairy plus cobwebby; leaf undersides stay visibly gray/green with both webby and short hairs, and berries are small and only slightly glaucous. Do not call a Central Texas Heller's grape V. cinerea solely because an app uses a broad species concept.

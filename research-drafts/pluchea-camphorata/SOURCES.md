## Morphology
LOOK HERE FIRST: alternate petiolate resinous leaves + rounded cluster of many small pink-purple discoid heads with no rays.

Priority confusers:
- Pluchea odorata — compare petiole prominence and involucre pubescence.
- Heterotheca subaxillaris — also called camphorweed, but has yellow radiate heads.

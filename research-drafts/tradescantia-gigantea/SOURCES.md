LOOK HERE FIRST
Start with size and lower-stem hairiness. Giant spiderwort is the robust, usually single-stemmed member of this trio, reaching about 90 cm in the Williamson treatment; the lower stem is essentially glabrous while the upper internodes become finely hairy. Its conspicuous paired leaflike bracts and relatively broad strap-shaped leaves reinforce the ID.

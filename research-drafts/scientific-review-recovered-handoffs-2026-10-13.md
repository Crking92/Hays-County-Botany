## Rhynchosia (four source names; three proposed learner concepts)
- *R. americana*: simple mature leaves; inspect several nodes because other Rhynchosia may bear simple basal leaves.
- *R. minima*: trifoliolate; compare racemes bearing multiple flowers (reported 5–13) with shorter/few-flowered axils of *R. senna*.
- *R. senna*: trifoliolate, usually short/few-flowered axillary flowering; historical var. *texana* treated as searchable synonym, not duplicate species (verify against current POWO).
- Unreliable alone: flower color, climbing habit, leaflet size/shape.
- Photo roles: several mature leaf nodes, complete flowering branch with raceme/axil, leaflets with scale, flowers and pods.
- Reported Sheet provenance: overrides 788–791; Master Plant Pool rows 980–983.

## Dichondra and Dichanthium (four source names)
- *Dichondra carolinensis* vs *D. recurvata*: compare fruiting pedicels in profile (reported straight vs sharply recurved distally), leaf underside pubescence (sparser vs tawny silky); sizes overlap.
- *Dichanthium annulatum* vs *D. sericeum*: both are introduced comparison grasses in Texas; investigate lower-glume hair placement on multiple mature sessile spikelets (upper flanks/ciliate keels vs transverse apical hair fringe). Spikelet size and awn length overlap. Require authoritative grass-flora confirmation before making these single-character rules definitive.
- Photo roles: matched fruiting pedicel side profiles with scale; leaf underside macro; matched sessile spikelet lower-glume macros with arrows; whole inflorescence.
- Reported Sheet provenance: overrides 792–795; Master Plant Pool rows 376–379.

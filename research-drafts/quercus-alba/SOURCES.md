## Strict target-zone range
US Forest Service native-range account places white oak broadly in the eastern United States, reaching eastern Texas:
https://www.srs.fs.usda.gov/pubs/misc/ag_654/volume_2/quercus/alba.htm

Texas Parks & Wildlife West Gulf Coastal Plain community material places Q. alba in East Texas forest communities such as Anderson, Bowie, Camp, Cass, Cherokee, Gregg, Harrison, Henderson, Nacogdoches, Panola, Rusk, Shelby, Smith, Upshur and other eastern counties:
https://tpwd.texas.gov/landwater/land/tcap/documents/tcap_wgcph_handbook.pdf

No authoritative native occurrence was located in three project target counties. The project's Hays native list does not include Q. alba.

Decision: FAIL — 0 firm target-zone counties in this review.

Guardrail: a source-pool iNaturalist record is occurrence evidence only and is not sufficient to prove target-zone nativity.

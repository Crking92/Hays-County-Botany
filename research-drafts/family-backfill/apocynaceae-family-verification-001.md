# Apocynaceae verification — Central Texas visual learner

**Status:** verified; ready for visual locking.

This is a light family pass because the species work is already strong. The project now has multiple canonical **Asclepias**, **Chthamalia biflora**, and **Matelea** packages, and Joey coverage is already good enough to teach the local milkweed/milkvine layer.

The main job here is hierarchy.

## Broad Apocynaceae first

Start with the broad family pattern:

- latex is common
- leaves commonly opposite or whorled, simple and entire
- stipules usually absent
- corolla fused, commonly 5-lobed
- five stamens associated closely with the corolla/stylar head
- superior ovary built from two carpels
- fruit often follicles, but broad-family fruit can also be capsule, berry or drupe

## Then teach the milkweed-side specialization

For our local Asclepias and milkvines, go straight to:

- **corona**
- **gynostegium**
- **pollinia / pollinarium**
- paired carpels and follicles
- silky comose seeds

Those are excellent local ID characters, but they are **not the definition of every Apocynaceae**.

That distinction is the main upgrade from the old nearly empty family file.

### Chthamalia biflora taxonomy
The live source pool still carries **Matelea biflora**. The canonical learner follows current Kew/POWO as **Chthamalia biflora** while retaining Matelea biflora as a synonym/alternative treatment. These are one learner concept, not two species.

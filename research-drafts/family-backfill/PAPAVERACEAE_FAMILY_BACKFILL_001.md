# Papaveraceae Family Backfill 001

## Where should I look first?

For local prickly poppies:

**latex → sepals → stamens → capsule**

Family pattern:
- colored/watery latex
- alternate exstipulate leaves
- 2–3 sepals that usually fall as the flower opens
- free petals
- many free stamens
- superior syncarpous ovary
- capsule

## Argemone is the local anchor

The plant is prickly and glaucous, but that alone is not the family diagnosis.

### Argemone aurantiaca
Do **not** use the white petals.

Look at the capsule:
- very unequal prickles
- longest commonly **branched**
- longest about **15–35 mm** in FNA treatment

### Argemone albiflora
Current POWO accepts the species and two subspecies.

The Texas name **A. albiflora var. texana** is now treated as **A. albiflora subsp. texana**.

Do not automatically relabel every local A. albiflora observation to the subspecies; review the actual specimen/photo first.

## Best family tie-breaker from Ranunculaceae

Papaveraceae:
**syncarpous capsule + latex + caducous sepals**

Ranunculaceae:
**mostly free carpels + aggregate achenes/follicles**

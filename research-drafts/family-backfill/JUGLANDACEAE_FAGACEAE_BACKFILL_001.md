# Juglandaceae vs Fagaceae Backfill 001

## The fastest split

### Juglandaceae / walnut
**Compound leaf + husked nut**

- alternate pinnately compound leaves
- often aromatic
- male catkins
- female flowers separate from male catkins
- hard nut inside a fleshy/fibrous husk

### Fagaceae / oak
**Simple leaf + cupule**

- alternate simple leaves
- male catkins
- nut/acorn
- acorn sits in a scaly cupule

## Don't use catkins alone
Both families can have male catkins.

## Local priority anchor: Juglans microcarpa

Look first:
1. many narrow leaflets on one compound leaf
2. small globose husked fruit

Then inspect catkins, buds, twig pith and nut grooves.

Fagaceae already has strong Joey + canonical oak coverage, so this module mainly closes the Juglandaceae side of the comparison.

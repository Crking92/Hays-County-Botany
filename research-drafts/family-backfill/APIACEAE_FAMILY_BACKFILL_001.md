# Apiaceae Family Backfill 001

## Where should I look first?

Start with the inflorescence, **but do not require an umbrella shape**.

Then confirm:
- sheathing leaf base
- inferior 2-carpellate ovary
- 2 styles / stylopodium
- dry fruit splitting into **2 mericarps**

## The local trap: Eryngium leavenworthii

It looks almost thistle-like.

That does **not** make it Asteraceae.

Its tiny flowers are packed into a dense spiny head, but its underlying reproductive architecture is Apiaceae.

This is the exact kind of plant that prevents the learner from overfitting to "carrot family = umbrella flower."

## Fast family distinction

**Apiaceae**
- umbels OR dense heads
- inferior ovary
- schizocarp → 2 mericarps

**Asteraceae**
- capitulum of florets
- involucre/phyllaries
- cypsela + often pappus

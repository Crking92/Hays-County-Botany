# Family recognition batch 001

Families: **Cyperaceae, Juncaceae, Boraginaceae, Solanaceae**

## Why this batch
This batch builds two high-value comparison systems:
1. **sedge vs rush vs grass**
2. **borage-family vs nightshade-family fused-flower plants**

## Core learning doors
- **Cyperaceae** — minute flower under one scale; superior one-ovule ovary; one-seeded nutlet. Carex adds a perigynium.
- **Juncaceae** — visible 6-tepal flower; 3 or 6 stamens; capsule with several seeds.
- **Boraginaceae** — broad POWO concept; choose among classic four-nutlet, hydrophylloid capsule, heliotrope, or woody Ehretia patterns.
- **Solanaceae** — fused usually 5-part flower; superior usually 2-carpellate many-ovuled ovary; berry or capsule.

## Visual priority
Cyperaceae: perigynium, Cyperus umbrella, Eleocharis terminal spikelet.
Juncaceae: 6-tepal flower, capsule, Juncus vs Luzula leaf margin.
Boraginaceae: scorpioid cyme, four nutlets, Phacelia/Nemophila capsule, anacua fruit.
Solanaceae: Solanum poricidal anthers, Physalis inflated calyx, Datura capsule, Nicotiana capsule.

No species are promoted by this family batch.

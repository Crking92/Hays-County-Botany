# Cactaceae verification — Central Texas visual learner

**Status:** verified; ready for visual locking.

## Where to look first
Find an **areole**. That is the strongest family cue. Areoles produce spines, glochids, hairs, branches and flowers.

Then read:
- ribs/tubercles or stem segments
- spines/glochids
- flower position
- inferior ovary
- berry-like many-seeded fruit

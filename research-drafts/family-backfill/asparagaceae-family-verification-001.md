# Asparagaceae verification — Central Texas visual learner

**Status:** verified; ready for visual locking.

Broad modern **Asparagaceae is heterogeneous**. That matters because our live source pool is currently all **Yucca**, while Joey also gives us Agave/Hesperaloe context.

## Broad family
Common reproductive framework:
- often 6 tepals
- usually 6 stamens
- 3-locular ovary
- ovary position can vary
- capsule or berry fruit

## Local Yucca lesson
Start with:
1. rosette/trunk architecture
2. stiff fibrous true leaves
3. terminal spine and margin fibers
4. terminal flower panicle
5. 3+3 tepals
6. six stamens
7. fruit/capsule

Teach this as **our local Yucca/Agavoideae-style module**, not the definition of every Asparagaceae.

## Taxonomy flag
The source pool contains both **Yucca treculeana** and **Yucca treculiana**. Current Kew accepts **Yucca treculiana**. Keep provenance but route the other spelling to taxonomic reconciliation rather than creating a duplicate learner concept.

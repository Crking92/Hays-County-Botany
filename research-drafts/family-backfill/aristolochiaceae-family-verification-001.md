# Aristolochiaceae verification — Central Texas visual learner

**Status:** verified; ready for visual locking.

## Where to look first
Go straight to the flower.

For local **Aristolochia**:
- alternate simple often cordate leaves
- one showy fused petaloid perianth/calyx tube
- no ordinary separate petal corolla
- inferior ovary
- anthers associated with the stylar column/gynostemium
- many-seeded capsule

The flower is a much stronger cue than vine habit because **Aristolochia erecta is erect**, not a big woody Dutchman's-pipe vine.

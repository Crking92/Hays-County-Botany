## Where to look first
For the local woody taxa:
- alternate/spiral leaves
- no stipules
- fused urn/bell/tubular corolla
- 5–10 stamens
- **poricidal anthers** are a high-value confirmation
- ovary position and fruit vary across the family

## Local pair
- **Arbutus xalapensis** — Texas madrone
- **Vaccinium arboreum** — sparkleberry/farkleberry

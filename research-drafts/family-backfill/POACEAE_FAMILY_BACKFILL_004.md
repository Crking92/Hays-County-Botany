# Poaceae Family Backfill 004 — Comparison Layer

## 1. Aristida purpurea vs Nassella leucotricha
**Look first: count the awns.**

- **Aristida purpurea:** three awns from the lemma.
- **Nassella leucotricha:** one conspicuous long twisted awn.

This is a much stronger field character than purple coloration or a generally wispy seedhead.

## 2. Panicum virgatum vs Sorghastrum nutans
**Look first: the collar region. Pull the blade away from the culm.**

- **Switchgrass:** dense white hairy collar/leaf-base area + membranous ligule.
- **Indiangrass:** upright paired horns — the classic rabbit ears / gun sight.

When flowering:
- switchgrass = large open airy panicle
- indiangrass = more contracted silky awned panicle

## 3. Grass vs sedge vs rush
**Look first: the stem.**

- **Grass:** joints/nodes.
- **Sedge:** often triangular, solid, no grass nodes.
- **Rush:** round and solid, no grass nodes; small flowers commonly show 6 tepals.

These are field shortcuts, not universal laws. Confirm with reproductive structures.

## 4. Two-door grass workflow

### Flowering?
Inflorescence → spikelet → glumes → floret → lemma/palea → awn → reproductive parts.

### Not flowering?
Node → blade/sheath junction → collar → ligule → auricles/horns → hairs → growth habit.

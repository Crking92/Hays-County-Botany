# Passifloraceae vs Vitaceae Backfill 001

## Ignore the leaf shape first. Look at the node.

### Passiflora / Passifloraceae
**Tendril = axillary.**

It arises beside the leaf/flower system in the leaf axil.

Then confirm the flower:
- corona filaments
- elevated reproductive column / androgynophore
- 5 stamens
- usually 3 conspicuous stigmas

### Vitis / Vitaceae
**Tendril = opposite the leaf.**

At the same node, the tendril or inflorescence is roughly 180° across from the leaf.

Then confirm:
- tiny flowers
- no passionflower corona
- petals often shed together like a cap in Vitis
- grape berry

## The killer comparison

> **Axillary tendril → think Passiflora.**  
> **Leaf-opposed tendril → think grape family.**

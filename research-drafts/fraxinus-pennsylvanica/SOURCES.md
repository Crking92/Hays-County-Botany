## Morphology / key comparison
UT PRC Central Texas ash key:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/DigFlora/ASH/FRAXI-CenTex.html

Key: usually 5–7(9) leaflets; weakly serrate/subentire margins; broad shield-shaped scar; consistently 2-winged samara with larger fruit body and wing running well down the body.

Hays warning: F. albicans and F. pennsylvanica can co-occur and possible hybridization is noted; prioritize fruit + leaf scar + lower-leaf surface.

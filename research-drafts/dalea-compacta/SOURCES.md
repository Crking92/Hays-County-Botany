## Taxonomy
Accepted: Dalea compacta Spreng.
Kew/POWO: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/490608-1

Kew accepts two infraspecific taxa:
- Dalea compacta var. compacta
- Dalea compacta var. pubescens (A.Gray) Barneby

Kew var. pubescens:
https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:76089-2

## Strict target-zone range
Barneby var. pubescens treatment:
https://sweetgum.nybg.org/science/world-flora/monographs-details/?irn=16215

Barneby describes the taxon as widespread and locally abundant across the eastern Texas Hill Country and cites specimens from:
- Hays County — Barneby 14503
- Blanco County — Barneby 14500
- Kerr County — A. Heller 1857

Decision: PASS (Hays, Blanco, Kerr)

Guardrail: Purola Preserve separately lists its local D. compacta as introduced at that specific site; that site record is not used for the range decision.

## Morphology / priority confuser
NPSOT:
https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/dalea-compacta/

Barneby:
https://sweetgum.nybg.org/science/world-flora/monographs-details/?irn=16215

Key: clumping herbaceous perennial; narrow linear leaflets; very dense swollen cylindrical/ovoid head. Dalea purpurea is the priority confuser; Barneby emphasizes calyx characters as the strongest differential rather than flower color.

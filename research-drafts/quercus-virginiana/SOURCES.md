## Taxon-concept / strict target-zone problem
Flora of North America Q. fusiformis treatment:
https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=233501031

FNA states typical Q. virginiana extends into Texas only as far west as the Brazos River drainage and along the coast to the Edwards Plateau escarpment; most live-oak populations farther west are intermediate or show greater affinity to Q. fusiformis.

Fort Cavazos modern native plant list uses Q. fusiformis for plateau live oak:
https://home.army.mil/hood/5917/3938/9979/2024-2029_Cavazos_INRMP_Final.pdf

Older Fort Hood material explicitly wrote "Quercus fusiformis (Q. virginiana)", illustrating the historic concept overlap:
https://home.army.mil/hood/download_file/view/7a8e2d26-2719-466c-aa7e-ea5eb08ccd14/920

Current decision: UNRESOLVED. Bell is retained as one modern target-county lead, but fewer than three target counties can presently be assigned confidently to Q. virginiana sensu stricto without specimen-level reconciliation.

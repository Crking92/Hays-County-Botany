LOOK HERE FIRST
Start with the fluffy head. Bushy bluestem makes dense, broomlike to cottony clusters near the top of the plant, with paired short rames packed inside and emerging from upper leaf sheaths. The inflorescence looks bushy rather than like separate turkey-foot fingers.

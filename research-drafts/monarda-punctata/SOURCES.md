https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/monarda-punctata/

Central-Texas M. stanfieldii comparison lead summarizing botanist Bill Carr's field distinction:
https://www.inaturalist.org/posts/7727-stanfield-s-beebalm-monarda-stanfieldii

LOOK HERE FIRST: cream/yellow purple-spotted corollas + pale pointed bracts without long bristles; then inspect the calyx mouth. M. punctata is open/ciliate, while M. stanfieldii has a dense white/silvery hair plug.

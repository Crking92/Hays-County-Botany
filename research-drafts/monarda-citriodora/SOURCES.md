## Morphology / ID
Texas Wildbuds description, based on regional floras; useful bract/calyx characters:
https://www.texaswildbuds.com/monarda-citriodora.html

NPSOT species page:
https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/monarda-citriodora/

LOOK HERE FIRST: stacked interrupted whorls + purple floral bracts + long bristle tips on the bracts/calyx teeth. Do not identify from purple flower color alone.

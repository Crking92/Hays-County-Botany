LOOK HERE FIRST
Start at the base of the plant. Texas spiderwort is low, many-stemmed and bushy at ground level, with clearly hairy vegetative shoots; toward the inflorescence the hairs become strongly glandular. That compact, fuzzy architecture separates it quickly from the taller giant and prairie spiderworts.

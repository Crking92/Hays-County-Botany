## Morphology
NPSOT above.

LOOK HERE FIRST: very narrow rough leaves + a usually single head with drooping rays and a hard prickly conical disk.

Priority confuser: Ratibida columnifera — elongated columnar disk + deeply divided leaves vs Echinacea's shorter prickly cone + mostly entire narrow leaves.

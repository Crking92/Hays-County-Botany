## Taxonomy

## Strict target-zone range
Travis — UT Plant Resources Center Rosaceae herbarium index documents three Travis County Prunus mexicana specimens:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/specimens/Rosaceae.html

Williamson — UT Plant Resources Center, Vascular Plants of Williamson County:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pages/Prunus%20mexicana.html

Comal — Harvard University Herbaria specimen GH 01722647, Prunus mexicana, collected at Anhalt, Texas:
https://kiki.huh.harvard.edu/databases/specimen_search.php?cltrid=42018&start=1

Handbook of Texas — Anhalt is in western Comal County:
https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/anhalt-tx

Decision: PASS — Travis, Williamson, Comal.

## Excluded evidence
Purola Preserve, Hays County explicitly labels its Prunus mexicana as introduced from a native plant nursery. It must not count as a wild/native Hays record:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/HaysFlora/pages/PRME.html

## Morphology
UT PRC Williamson County treatment:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pages/Prunus%20mexicana.html

Key learner sequence:
- small deciduous tree + bark/lenticels
- alternate stipulate serrate leaves
- short spur-shoot flower clusters
- 5 petals + hypanthium + many free stamens
- one superior carpel
- one glaucous single-stone drupe

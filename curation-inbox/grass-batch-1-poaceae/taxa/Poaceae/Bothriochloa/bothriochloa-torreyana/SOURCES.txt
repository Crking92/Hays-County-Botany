IMPORTANT TAXONOMIC MAPPING
Many Texas references use Bothriochloa laguroides subsp. torreyana (Steud.) Allred & Gould.
The learner uses the current accepted Kew name Bothriochloa torreyana (Steud.) Scrivanti & Anton while preserving the older Texas name as a synonym.

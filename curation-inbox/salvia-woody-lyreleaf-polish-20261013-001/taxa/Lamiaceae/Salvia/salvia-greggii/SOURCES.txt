Salvia greggii autumn sage, approved existing card. Preserve original and native range UNRESOLVED (not 3-county PASS).
Botanical key from UT Austin: https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pdf/Salvia%20greggii
Leaves about 8-48 mm, fairly smooth/glabrous with minute pitted glands; ~25 mm larger flower, wild typically crimson/rose-red; cultivated colors vary and hybrids S. microphylla/S. x jamensis may resemble it.

Identification: LOOK HERE FIRST: Check the pinnately compound leaves with smooth-edged oval leaflets and the paired short stipular spines at twig nodes. In flower, look for drooping clusters of white pea-shaped blossoms; later look for flat pods. Unlike honey locust (Gleditsia triacanthos), black locust typically has paired node spines rather than large branched trunk thorns.

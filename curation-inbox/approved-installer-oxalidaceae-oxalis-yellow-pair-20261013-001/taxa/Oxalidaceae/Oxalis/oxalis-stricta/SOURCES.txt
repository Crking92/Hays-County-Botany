Taxon: Oxalis stricta L.
Source-pool: Master Plant Pool row 803; learner-approved, not a confirmed three-county native.
Taxonomy / morphology: Flora of North America, https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=242416902
Taxonomy / range: Plants of the World Online, Kew; see Master Plant Pool taxonomy review for accepted concept and Texas caveat.
Range status: CONFLICT; FNA and POWO do not list Texas as native for this accepted concept, whereas the project Hays source pool includes it. Texas native status and source ID remain a voucher-level conflict; no target-zone PASS.
Morphological distinction: O. dillenii usually has straight forward-appressed nonseptate hairs, fewer flowers (often 1–3), and longer 12–20 mm capsules. O. albicans has short curved/crisped nonseptate hairs; O. corniculata may root at prostrate nodes and has conspicuous broad free stipules.

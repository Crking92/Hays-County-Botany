Chaetopappa ericoides — approved Master Plant Pool row 239; source-pool learner admission separate from county-native certification.
Reviewed identity in Master Plant Pool 2026-10-10. POWO: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:274379-2
Flora of North America Chaetopappa key: https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=106357
Key diagnostic comparison: C. effusa has broad flat non-overlapping lower leaves and a minute pappus ring; C. bellidifolia is a taprooted annual with flat leaves and a tiny ring. C. hersheyi also has overlapping leathery leaves but much shorter stems and a two-series pappus with only about five long inner bristles plus outer scales.

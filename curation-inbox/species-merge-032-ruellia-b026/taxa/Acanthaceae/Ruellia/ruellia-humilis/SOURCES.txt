LOOK HERE FIRST
Look for obvious individual hairs before looking at the flower. Prairie petunia is conspicuously long-pilose on stems, leaves and especially leaf margins, and its flowers are axillary and nearly sessile. That shaggy/ciliate look separates it from finely pubescent R. drummondiana and mostly glabrous terminal-flowered R. ciliatiflora.

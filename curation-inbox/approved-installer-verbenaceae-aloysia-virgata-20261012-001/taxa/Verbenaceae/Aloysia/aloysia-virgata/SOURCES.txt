Aloysia virgata — approved Master Plant Pool row 38.
Accepted identity: Kew Plants of the World Online; synonym Verbena virgata. Native range South America. Preserve NON_NATIVE_COMPARISON and exclude native-only metrics.
Diagnostic lesson: existing canonical Aloysia genus.json. Measure mature opposite leaves and compare loose zigzag racemes with A. gratissima's shorter crowded racemes.

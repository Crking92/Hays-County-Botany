Cercis canadensis subsp. texensis — B124 visual lock

Taxonomy:
- Current Kew POWO accepts Cercis canadensis subsp. texensis (S.Watson) A.E.Murray.
- Cercis canadensis var. texensis is a synonym of the accepted subspecies.

Range:
- Existing B085 PASS retained: Hays, Travis, Williamson.
- Williamson County UT Plant Resources Center treatment documents the legacy var. texensis concept now mapped to the accepted subspecies.

Identification:
- Thick glossy leathery broad leaves, generally larger and less wavy-edged than Mexican redbud.
- Pink-purple flowers occur on older wood before/with leaf emergence.

Visuals:
- Two exact private-study image leads selected for diagnostic foliage and spring growth/flowers.
- Reuse rights remain review-needed before any future public release.

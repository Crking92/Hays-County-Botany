Cercis canadensis subsp. mexicana — B124 visual lock

Taxonomy:
- Current Kew POWO accepts Cercis canadensis subsp. mexicana (Rose) A.E.Murray.
- Cercis canadensis var. mexicana and Cercis mexicana are synonyms.

Range:
- Existing B085 FAIL/comparison status retained under the project three-county threshold.
- Kew native range: southwestern Texas to northeastern Mexico.

Identification:
- Smaller, thick glossy leaves with characteristically stronger wavy/undulate margins than Texas redbud.
- Compact xeric tree/large-shrub habit; spring flowers before or with expanding leaves.

Visuals:
- Two exact private-study image leads selected for strongly wavy foliage and flower/leaf habit.

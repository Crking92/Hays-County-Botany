Erigeron modestus A.Gray, Plains fleabane. Approved Master Plant Pool row 433, reviewed 2026-10-11.
Flora of North America: https://floranorthamerica.org/Erigeron_modestus
Kew Plants of the World Online: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:204164-1
Identification: thick woody root crown and old basal stems; heads vary from early large to later multiple smaller; ray number 24-65 (occasionally 170), ray length 4-7 (occasionally 11) mm, involucre 3-4.5 mm. Compare annual slender-rooted E. geiseri. FNA describes a variable polyploid complex and some identifications require vouchers.
Synonyms include E. plateauensis and E. lobatus var. warnockii; do not install those as distinct species.
Target-zone county native status UNRESOLVED. Source-pool approval permits learner inclusion but not confirmed-native counting. Exact-species photo backfill pending.

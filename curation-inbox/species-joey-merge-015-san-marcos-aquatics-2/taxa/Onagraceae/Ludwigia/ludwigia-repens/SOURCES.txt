MORPHOLOGY
FNA and UT PRC Williamson treatment: creeping aquatic perennial, roots at nodes, opposite leaves, glossy elliptic/obovate blades, small axillary flowers.

LOOKALIKE WARNING
Hygrophila polysperma has historically been confused with L. repens in the San Marcos/Comal systems. Treat submerged vegetative ID cautiously when flowers/fruits are absent.

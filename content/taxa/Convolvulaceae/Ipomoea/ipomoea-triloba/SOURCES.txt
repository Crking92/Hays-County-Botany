IPOMOEA TRILOBA — CURATION SOURCES
Reviewed: 2026-09-30

PROJECT MODE
Local-occurrence-first. Strict native filtering is deferred.

TAXONOMY / DISTRIBUTION
Kew Plants of the World Online:
https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:129763-2
Accepted: Ipomoea triloba L.
Kew treats Texas as introduced.

MORPHOLOGY
Flora of North America:
https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=200018860

Important FNA characters:
- annual
- usually twining
- leaf blades orbiculate/broadly ovate or 3–7-lobed
- base cordate
- sepals about 6–7 mm long
- margins ciliate
- lavender funnelform corolla about 10–20 mm long

Kew general description also supports:
- slender twining habit
- cordate, often 3–5-lobed leaves
- sepals ca. 6 mm
- pink/rose flowers
- subglobose capsules

KEY COMPARISON
Ipomoea hederacea:
- sepals usually 12–24 mm and prolonged into long narrow tips
Ipomoea triloba:
- sepals much shorter, about 6–7 mm
- corolla much smaller

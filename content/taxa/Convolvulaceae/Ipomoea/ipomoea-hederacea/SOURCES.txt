TAXONOMY / DISTRIBUTION
Kew Plants of the World Online:
https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:268998-1
Accepted: Ipomoea hederacea Jacq.
Kew currently treats the native range as Mexico and Texas as introduced.

MORPHOLOGY
Flora of North America:
https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=230002816

Important FNA characters:
- annual, twining stems
- leaves orbiculate/ovate or 3–5-lobed
- leaf surfaces hairy
- hairy peduncles with retrorse hairs
- sepals 12–24 mm
- sepal base broadened, then abruptly narrowed to a long curved/spreading tip
- light-blue funnel-shaped corolla
- flowering May–November

LOOK-ALIKE SUPPORT
A foundation monograph of Ipomoea in the New World:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7298354/

For I. hederacea vs. I. nil, the key emphasizes corolla size, sepal length,
recurving sepal tips and peduncle length.

For I. triloba, shorter sepals and a much smaller corolla are useful contrasts.

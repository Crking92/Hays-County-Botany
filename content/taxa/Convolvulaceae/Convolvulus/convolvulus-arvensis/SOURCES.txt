PHOTO METHOD
The genus-level downloader queries the iNaturalist public API.
It first requests curated taxon photos, then prefers a multi-photo research-grade observation
from the Central Texas bounding box. If none is available, it falls back to Texas.
The resulting image-sources.csv stores role, observation/taxon source, attribution,
license code, and direct image URL.

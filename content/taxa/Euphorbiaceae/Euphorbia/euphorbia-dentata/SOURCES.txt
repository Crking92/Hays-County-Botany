Diagnostic comparison
Use abaxial hair texture, capsule size, and mature seed:
E. dentata = longer weak filiform hairs; smaller capsules; smaller rounded-in-cross-section evenly minute-tuberculate seeds.
E. davidii = shorter stiffer hairs; larger capsules/seeds; angular irregularly tuberculate seeds.

Key diagnostic characters
- usually erect to ascending, rarely prostrate
- stems/leaves papillate
- foliage usually villous but sometimes glabrous
- stipules distinct and filiform
- capsule glabrous; cocci often elongated with an empty distal portion
- seeds weakly 4-angled, smooth to faintly rugose

Diagnostic comparison
E. davidii: shorter/stiffer often pustule-based hairs; larger capsules; larger angular irregularly tuberculate seeds.
E. dentata: longer weak filiform hairs; smaller capsules; smaller rounded evenly minute-tuberculate seeds.

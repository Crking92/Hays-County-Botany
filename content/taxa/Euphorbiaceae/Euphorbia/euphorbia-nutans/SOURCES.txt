UT Austin Chamaesyce diagnostic page:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/CHAMA-D/html/CHNU9.html

Key characters
- ascending to erect annual, branch tips often arched
- relatively large opposite oblong leaves
- strongly asymmetric leaf bases and serrulate margins
- red blotch common but not required
- glabrous capsule
- dark finely wrinkled seeds

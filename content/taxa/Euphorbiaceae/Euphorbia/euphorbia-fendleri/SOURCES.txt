Key characters
- perennial with woody thickened taproot/crown
- low clustered glabrous stems
- small rounded entire glabrous leaves
- distinct narrow-linear stipules
- glabrous capsule
- white prominently 4-angled seeds
- gland appendage shape is variable and should not drive identification

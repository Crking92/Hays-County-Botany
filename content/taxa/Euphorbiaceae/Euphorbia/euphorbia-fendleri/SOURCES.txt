Key characters
- perennial with woody thickened taproot/crown
- low clustered glabrous stems
- small rounded entire glabrous leaves
- distinct narrow-linear stipules
- glabrous capsule
- white prominently 4-angled seeds
- gland appendage shape is variable and should not drive identification

## Specialty macro follow-up — 2026-10-08

images/03-travis-voucher.jpg
Role: Herbarium voucher / thickened rootstock and crown context
Visual cue and limit: Look at the central and lower-left mounted plants, where several stems meet a thickened crown and a taproot extends below it. The voucher supplies crown/rootstock context with the original ruler retained; it does not establish living color, plant age or internal wood anatomy.
Source and reuse status unchanged: UT voucher; private study; public reuse review.

images/06-stipule-macro.jpg
UT Austin / Bob Harms; reuse rights unverified

https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/CHAMA-D/html/lrg/CHFE3/CHFE3-stipl-3x-w.jpg
Role: Stipule macro / narrow paired stipules with upper branches
Visual cue and limit: Find the pale narrow stipules rising close together at the leaf bases. Their upper portions have hairlike branches. Compare these with the broad fused scale in E. serpens; closeness alone does not demonstrate fusion. The original 1 mm scale bar is retained.
Study use: yes; public reuse: review

Role: Seed macro / multiple faces and calibrated scale
Visual cue and limit: Compare the four pale seeds on the dark background: different faces reveal the angular form and low surface relief. The author specifies 0.5 mm scale divisions. Fine surface texture is visible at this magnification; do not confuse it with the strong transverse ridges of E. prostrata. Physiological maturity is not explicitly documented.
Study use: yes; public reuse: no

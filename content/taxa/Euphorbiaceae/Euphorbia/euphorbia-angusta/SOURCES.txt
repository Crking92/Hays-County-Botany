Morphology
Flora of North America:
https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=250101551

Key characters
- perennial from moderately to strongly thickened rootstock
- erect stems 12–43 cm, uniformly strigose
- distal leaves linear to elliptic-linear and more than 6× as long as wide
- involucre and capsule strigose
- white flabellate gland appendages shallowly/irregularly toothed
- white 4-angled seeds with low transverse ridges or wrinkles

Smilax bona-nox L. — saw greenbrier — approved Master Plant Pool row 1088.
Accepted species, family Smilacaceae: Kew Plants of the World Online https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:541314-1 . Do not conflate with other Smilax taxa or infraspecific synonym concepts.
LOOK HERE FIRST morphology: green climbing prickly stems, coiling tendrils emerging from petiole region, variable leathery triangular/hastate leaves with strong veins arising from base.

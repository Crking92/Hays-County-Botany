Alternanthera philoxeroides (Mart.) Griseb. — accepted taxon, approved Master Plant Pool row 40 (My iNaturalist Flora source pool, taxon 75386). POWO/WCVP review in Master Plant Pool: native Trinidad to northern Argentina; Texas introduced. Learner role NON_NATIVE_COMPARISON; no confirmed-target-native count. No unsupported invasive label.
Morphology-first field cue: opposite leaves at stem nodes; stalked globose white flower heads; wetland or floating mats. Compare A. caracasana compact heads and A. pungens sharp bracts. Habitat is supportive, not definitive.

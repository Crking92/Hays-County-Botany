## Morphology
UT PRC Williamson County treatment:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pages/Opuntia%20macrorhiza.html

Key learner cues:
- low radiating/sprawling habit
- relatively small pads
- areoles with orange-brown glochids
- few permanent spines, often with a strongly bent/deflexed main spine
- large yellow flower with reddish/rose-red basal pigmentation
- inferior ovary and fleshy many-seeded fruit

## Comparison guardrail
Do not identify this species from one spine count. Compare habit, pad dimensions, areole/glochid pattern and spine posture together against O. engelmannii var. lindheimeri and other Opuntia.

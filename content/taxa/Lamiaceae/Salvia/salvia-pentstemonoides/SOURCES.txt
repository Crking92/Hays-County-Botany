## Taxonomy
Accepted: Salvia pentstemonoides Kunth & C.D.Bouché
Accepted spelling is Salvia pentstemonoides (extra t). U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service explicitly notes that penstemonoides is a common misspelling. Preserve the Master Plant Pool source spelling for provenance but use pentstemonoides for the canonical taxon.


## Strict project-range evidence
- Bandera — NPSOT historical/rediscovery account documents wild populations in Bandera County.
- Real — NPSOT account explicitly documents a private-land wild population in Real County.
- Kerr — NPSOT reports rediscovery of a large wild population along the Guadalupe River in Kerr County; historical Kerr specimens are also noted.
- Gillespie — U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service identifies the 1845 upper Pedernales type material from Gillespie County as historical backup evidence.
Decision: PASS

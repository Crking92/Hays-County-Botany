Salvia lyrata lyreleaf sage existing card retained with range UNRESOLVED. Target first leaf at ground level. One LYRATE blade has broad rounded terminal lobe and smaller lateral lobes, not separate leaflets. Some individuals shallowly lobed; pigment not decisive. Match to cedar sage which may have up to three separate leaflets.

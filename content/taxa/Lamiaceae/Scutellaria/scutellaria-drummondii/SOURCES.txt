## Morphology
UT PRC Williamson County treatment above.

Key measurements:
- annual, 15–30 cm
- acute-tipped leaves; lower leaves low-crenate to upper entire
- pedicel ca. 1–1.2 mm at anthesis
- visibly glandular-hairy calyx/scutellum
- corolla ca. 5.5–10 mm

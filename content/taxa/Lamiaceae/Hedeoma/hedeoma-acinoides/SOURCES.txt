Hedeoma acinoides Scheele — approved Master Plant Pool row 530, reviewed 2026-10-12. Source-pool approval is independent of target-county certification.
Taxonomy: POWO accepted Hedeoma acinoides Scheele; https://powo.science.kew.org/results?q=Hedeoma%20acinoides
Morphology: Illustrated Flora of North Central Texas Hedeoma key and description, pp. 758-761: https://brit.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/FNCT_0618-1076-Fab-Zygo.pdf
Texas field description: https://www.texaswildbuds.com/hedeoma-acinoides.html
Calyx lower third saccate, calyx ca. 5-6 mm; corolla pink ca. 9-13 mm, noninflated. Small annual with opposite oval leaves; compare narrow-leaved H. drummondii with closing mature calyx teeth and woody-based H. reverchonii. Project native-range status UNRESOLVED; exclude from confirmed native-only totals.

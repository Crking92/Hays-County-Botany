## Morphology / field recognition
Native Plant Society of Texas — Virginia Dutchman's Pipe / Virginia Snakeroot:
https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/aristolochia-serpentaria/

Key training sequence:
- low woodland herb; leaves variable lanceolate to cordate
- flower arises low on slender/scaly peduncle
- petals absent; showy structure is tubular petaloid calyx/perianth
- inferior ovary
- nearly spherical dry capsule

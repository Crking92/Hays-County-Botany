Role: Flower macro — smooth filaments and lobed yellow staminodes
Visual cue: Look at the smooth, curved filaments below the petals and the small lobed yellow staminodes above them. Commelina erecta has three fertile stamens plus three staminodes. The dense purple-and-yellow filament tufts of Tinantia are absent. Do not count the pistil as a stamen.

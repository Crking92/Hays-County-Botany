Role: Joey macro — purple-and-yellow beards on stamen filaments
Visual cue: The purple-and-yellow tufts are hairs on the filaments, not extra petals. Tinantia has six fertile stamens; five filaments are bearded, with the lower central filament smooth. Compare this image directly with Commelina erecta's smooth filament macro. The little white lower petal is also visible.

The boatlike green coverings immediately around individual buds are sepals; do not confuse those with a spathe.

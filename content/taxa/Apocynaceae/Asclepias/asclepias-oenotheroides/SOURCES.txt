MORPHOLOGY / TEXAS FIELD IDENTIFICATION
Texas Parks & Wildlife Department — Identification of Milkweeds in Texas
https://tpwd.texas.gov/publications/pwdpubs/media/pwd_rp_w7000_1803.pdf
Zizotes milkweed treatment:
- 1–5 dm tall
- stems branched from base, ascending or decumbent
- opposite/sub-opposite leaves
- ovate leaves with wavy margins
- lateral inflorescences
- long slender hoods extending above stigmatic groove
- greenish-white or yellow corolla
- April–November flowering
- long slender hoods described as unique among milkweeds in the guide

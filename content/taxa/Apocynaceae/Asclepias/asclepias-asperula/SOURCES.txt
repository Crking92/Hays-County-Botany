MORPHOLOGY / FIELD IDENTIFICATION
Texas Parks & Wildlife — Identification of Milkweeds in Texas
https://tpwd.texas.gov/publications/pwdpubs/media/pwd_rp_w7000_1803.pdf
Antelopehorns treatment is on page 8.
Supports:
- stout rootstocks
- clustered ascending/decumbent stems
- lanceolate to linear-lanceolate leaves
- crowded terminal inflorescences
- pale-yellowish flowers
- April–September flowering
- comparison with Asclepias viridis

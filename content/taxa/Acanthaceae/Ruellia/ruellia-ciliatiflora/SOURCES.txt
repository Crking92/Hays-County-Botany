LOOK HERE FIRST
Start with where the flowers are. Common wild petunia sends a conspicuous peduncled, branching terminal flower stalk above the leaves; the flowers are not sitting directly in the leaf axils. Then confirm mostly glabrous/glabrescent oval-ovate leaves with wavy to low-dentate margins.

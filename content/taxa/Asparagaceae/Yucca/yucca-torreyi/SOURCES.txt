## Taxonomy
Accepted: Yucca torreyi Shafer
- Kew/POWO current accepted treatment: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A293670-2
- 2025 Yucca phylogeny cited by Kew: https://doi.org/10.11646/phytotaxa.687.2.2
Taxonomic note: Flora of North America (2002) cited this under Yucca treculeana; current Kew/POWO accepts Y. torreyi separately.

## Strict project-range evidence
- Burnet — Illustrated Flora of North Central Texas records Y. torreyi in Burnet County; field documentation at Longhorn Cavern State Park also places flowering Y. torreyi in Burnet County.
- Bexar — Government Canyon State Natural Area vegetation documentation includes Y. torreyi among local succulents.
- Val Verde — Amistad National Recreation Area vascular plant inventory lists Y. torreyi in Val Verde County.
Decision: PASS

## Morphology / separation from Y. treculiana
- Illustrated Flora key: https://fwbg.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/SBM_26-pp309-1151.pdf
- NPSOT: https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/yucca-torreyi/
Traditional key: marginal threads + apical leaf portion often inrolled + slender ovary <= ~7 mm. Use several characters because older treatments lumped the pair and hybridization has been reported.

Senna roemeriana — B123 web install

Taxonomy:
- Kew Plants of the World Online: accepted Senna roemeriana (Scheele) H.S.Irwin & Barneby.
- Synonyms include Cassia roemeriana Scheele and Earleocassia roemeriana (Scheele) Britton.

Project target-zone evidence:
- Hays County: UT Plant Resources Center Purola Preserve natural-flora list.
- Williamson County: UT Plant Resources Center Vascular Plants of Williamson County treatment.
- Kerr County: New York Botanical Garden / Barneby Catalogue voucher, A.A. Heller 1666, Kerrville, Kerr County.

Identification and visuals:
- UT Austin BIO406D Senna roemeriana teaching page.
- Exact real photos selected for flower, diagnostic two-leaflet leaf, whole habit, and fruit.
- Learner overlay should point to the one leaflet pair and interfoliolar gland on the leaf image.

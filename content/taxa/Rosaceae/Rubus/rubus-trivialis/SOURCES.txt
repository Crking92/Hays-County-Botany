## Taxonomy
Kew Plants of the World Online — accepted Rubus trivialis Michx.; native in Texas:
https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:740944-1

Taxonomy caution: Rubus trivialis Pursh is a later illegitimate homonym and is treated as a synonym of Rubus flagellaris; the learner uses R. trivialis Michx.

Key family-learning sequence:
- stipulate alternate 3–5-foliolate leaf
- prickly trailing/arching cane
- shallow hypanthium
- many free stamens + many free superior pistils
- aggregate of fleshy drupelets

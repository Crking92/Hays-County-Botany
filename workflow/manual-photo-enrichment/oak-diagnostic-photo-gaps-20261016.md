## Capture and curation requirements
- Prefer living, verified plants photographed in natural light, with **leaf upper and underside, petiole/twig, bud, acorn and whole-tree habit** when available.
- Collect several organs from **one same-tree observation** if possible. Independent iNaturalist labels can be provisional, even with exact Latin spelling.
- Record original source, individual creator, actual photo-level Creative Commons license, observation link and original scientific name. For Joey Santore CC BY-NC material, preserve private/noncommercial-only restrictions.
- Never substitute one oak species or cultivar for another, change range status, force taxonomy holds, or draw fine anatomical arrows on unidentified material.
- Use **BONAP/NAPA and target-county evidence separately** for nativity decisions; this photo task makes no native-range claims.

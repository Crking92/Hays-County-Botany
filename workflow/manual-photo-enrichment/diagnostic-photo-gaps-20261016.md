### Collection and import rules
- Favor specimens from Hays County and nearby target counties, but don't treat location alone as an identification.
- Prioritize several organs from **the same individual plant**, with scale.
- Use the user's own iNaturalist photographs and Joey Santore's CC BY-NC images when actually credited and suitable; retain individual licensing and noncommercial constraints. Other GBIF/iNaturalist/Pl@ntNet media require rights checks.
- Never change nativity, county classification, accepted name, or taxonomic HOLD based on photograph source labels.
- Avoid duplicate images and keep original image provenance. New images remain provisional until independent taxonomic and fine morphological confirmation.

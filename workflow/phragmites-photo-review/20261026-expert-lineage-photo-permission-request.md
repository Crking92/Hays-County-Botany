## Interpretation caveat: 2012 lower-glume prose inconsistency

The 2012 Florida paper states in text that the Eurasian lower glumes were **longer**, while immediately reporting *Eurasian 3.92 ± 0.12 mm* and *Gulf Coast 4.38 ± 0.05 mm*. The **numbers imply the opposite**. Do not turn that apparent wording inconsistency into a field cutoff; both groups overlap. The Gulf/Eurasian ligules were not significantly different (study mean approximately 0.9 mm). Reference: https://apms.org/wp-content/uploads/japm-50-01-69.pdf (original article page 70/results text and page 73 discussion).

## Proposed request

Subject: Request to use three North Hays King Ranch bluestem anatomy photos for free teaching site

Hello Mr. Harms,

I'm working on a publicly accessible, noncommercial Central Texas grass-identification teaching resource for restoration crews and other learners. Your detailed photographs of King Ranch bluestem (*Bothriochloa ischaemum* var. *songarica*) from northern Hays County are unusually valuable for showing the exact structures that distinguish it from native bluestems.

Would you grant permission to copy and store the following three original DigFlora photographs in the project's GitHub source repository and display them on its free GitHub Pages website? We would like to resize/crop them for mobile display and add carefully checked arrow labels identifying visible structures. Every use would visibly credit “Bob Harms,” link to your original source, and state any usage conditions you specify. Your copyright would remain yours; we would not describe the photographs as Creative Commons without your explicit direction.

If you prefer only external links, no modifications, or other specific attribution/usage restrictions, we'd be happy to honor them. A brief written reply stating the permitted forms of use would be enough for our photo-rights record.

Thank you for making such detailed Central Texas grass identification material available.

## Existing species fruit-photo repair
- `L. octovalvis`: [Alex Abair, CC BY 4.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ludwigia_octovalvis_50612016.jpg), actual *green developing* elongated ribbed capsule with persistent sepals; no scale. Now present as an external gallery reference on the canonical species.
- `L. repens`: [Correll & Correll 1972 botanical plate / fig. 562](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aquatic_and_wetland_plants_of_southwestern_United_States_(1972)_(19562736390).jpg), public-domain US per Commons. Shows real diagnostic shapes as **drawings**, including short mature fruit `f`, seed `g`, creeping rooted habit `a`. Not a real photo macro.
- **Rejected:** [Commons photograph titled Ludwigia repens with water root](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ludwigia_repens_with_a_water_root_-_20091016.jpg), because original uploader file history calls it **Ludwigia ovalis**. Do not use as exact-species repens.

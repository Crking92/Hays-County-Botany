# Calvin King / Glass Root Garden photograph permission

On 2026-10-14, Calvin confirmed in this project chat that the photographs from his iNaturalist account are his, that he updated their copyright settings after earlier downloads, and that he wants them used to fill blank learner cards. He explicitly authorized continued use in his private Hays County botany learner.

This records **direct photo-owner permission for private learner use**. It does not assert that iNaturalist currently displays CC BY on every individual photograph, does not grant rights to photographs credited to someone else, and does not change the licenses of any already-installed photo.

For each new photo, verify observer `calvinking`, exact accepted species or documented synonym, individual photo ID and owner attribution (Calvin King / Glass Root Garden). Preserve observed API copyright metadata separately. Credit Glass Root Garden. If the source has a verified public CC license, record it accurately; otherwise mark public_reuse=no and owner-authorized private study. Strip embedded EXIF/GPS. Keep scientific/range decisions and existing galleries intact.

The existing Google Sheet has source taxon IDs and example observations. The Photo Coverage Log adds per-photo observation IDs, photo IDs, rights basis, source status, exact installed path, and verification state. A source lead is not an installed image.

## Photo-first sequence per existing species
1. **Whole habit**: actual rooted plant / clump / spreading runner; growing and mature stage if distinctive.
2. **Leaves**: blade, true upper/lower surfaces, sheath, keel, **the actual ligule at blade/sheath junction**, collar, hairs with their correct attached structures; capture mm scale for small parts.
3. **Flowering architecture**: intact stem traced to each flowering branch and sheath, photographed before and after seed maturity.
4. **Detailed reproductive anatomy**: intact same-plant spikelet(s) at 10–20× or better, **glumes versus lemmas, sessile versus pedicellate partner, awn origin, anthers/stigmas, rachis hairs/disarticulation** when relevant; actual ruler/scale.
5. **Look-alikes**: correctly determined real examples of 1–3 local closest species at comparable angles and growth stages; mark why, not just names.
6. **Crew teach-back**: high-value photo/arrows/recognition cue; species field ID then the comparison, then genus/family lesson at end. Existing quiz/ID mechanics stay untouched.

Use existing vetted physical photo archive/library first. Discover external *specific* licensed photos only for roles truly missing or materially better. Require exact scientific identity, source and creator, image-level license/publicity (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA; correctly preserve NC restrictions within the current noncommercial rights-filtered site; confirmed photographer-specific permission also allowed). Preserve every original source and modification record. No unknown rights, private-only uploads or falsely transferred license. Do not conflate synonym or variety concepts.

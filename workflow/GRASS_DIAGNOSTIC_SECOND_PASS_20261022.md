2. **Leaves**: blade, true upper/lower surfaces, sheath, keel, **the actual ligule at blade/sheath junction**, collar, hairs with their correct attached structures; capture mm scale for small parts.
3. **Flowering architecture**: intact stem traced to each flowering branch and sheath, photographed before and after seed maturity.

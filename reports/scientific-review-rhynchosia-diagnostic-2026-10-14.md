## Accepted names
- R. americana (Mill.) Metz accepted (POWO); Rhynchosia menispermoides and Lathyrus americanus synonyms.
- R. minima (L.) DC. accepted (POWO); Dolichos minimus synonym.
- R. senna Gillies ex Hook. & Arn. accepted (POWO); R. senna var. texana synonym, retain as searchable historical label, not fourth card. Other authorities sometimes retain variety; preserve original determination.

## LOOK HERE FIRST
1. At several mature nodes, count actual leaflets: R. americana normally has simple heart-shaped leaves, R. minima and R. senna three leaflets. One juvenile/basal leaf is not conclusive.
2. Follow the entire flowering axil: UT Williamson County reports R. minima in open 5–13-flowered racemes; Arizona Cooperative Extension describes R. senna flowers singly or few together on short axillary stalks. Count several intact racemes; missing flowers can mislead.
3. UT R. minima leaflets may be rhombic/obovate to 30 x 30 mm; Arizona R. senna often has relatively narrow leaflets. Leaf shape overlaps. Yellow flower color and twining habit are not diagnostic.
4. UT R. minima mature pods 14–21 x 2.5–4 mm; Arizona R. senna pods to approximately 19 mm. Fruit length overlaps. Pair pod with intact flowering node.

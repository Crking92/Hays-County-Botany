# Medicago scientific review (2026-10-12)

Source-pool rows 698–700, no canonical installation. Accepted POWO: Medicago lupulina L., M. polymorpha L., M. sativa L. All are introduced in Texas, learner-approved non-native comparisons.

LOOK HERE FIRST: examine mature fruit, not yellow flowers or trifoliate leaves.
- M. lupulina: numerous small yellow flowers in a dense head; each small, kidney-shaped, blackening, unarmed one-seeded pod. Confusable with yellow Trifolium, but inspect individual fruit.
- M. polymorpha: few yellow flowers, mature multi-seeded pod coiled 2–7 turns; usually hooked prickles, but spineless forms exist. Synonyms include M. hispida and M. denticulata; avoid separate cards.
- M. sativa: usually erect perennial alfalfa, purple to blue-violet flowers, unarmed pod typically 2–3 coils. Yellow-flowered relatives and hybrids mean color alone is unreliable.

PHOTO ROLES: dissected mature fruit of each at same scale, show number of coils, spines, and seeds; complete flower clusters; leaflet apex and stipules; M. lupulina vs yellow Trifolium fruit. No photos verified.

# Grass diagnostic caution — 2026-10-14

In the Digitaria ciliaris versus D. cognata comparison, whole inflorescence architecture is more reliable than overlapping spikelet lengths. D. ciliaris has fingerlike racemes and paired spikelets along the rachis; D. cognata has an open, spreading panicle. Inspect the branch bases, ligule and mature spikelets. In grass descriptions, 'single-flowered' may mean one fertile floret even when a sterile lower floret is also present. For Echinochloa crus-galli, absent ligule and mature fertile-lemma tip are more informative than highly variable awn length. Eleusine indica has multiple florets per spikelet. These are photo-role research notes, not installed media or canonical admission changes.

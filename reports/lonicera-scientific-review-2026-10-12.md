# Lonicera scientific diagnostic review — 2026-10-12

Review only: no canonical species, READY marker, or gallery installation. Reviewed existing genus/family modules, manifest, claims, research queue and live Master Plant Pool rows 657–659.

Accepted names (POWO 2026):
- Lonicera albiflora Torr. & A.Gray — Texas native at state level; target three-county PASS unresolved; approved learner pending curation. L. dumosa is a synonym.
- Lonicera japonica Thunb. — introduced in Texas, Texas Invasive Plant Inventory rates Moderate; approved invasive encounter comparison, excluded from native totals.
- Lonicera sempervirens L. — Texas native at state level; target three-county PASS unresolved; approved learner pending curation.

LOOK HERE FIRST: trace inflorescence insertion. Paired axillary two-flowered cymes suggest L. japonica; terminal whorls suggest L. albiflora or L. sempervirens. Measure terminal corollas: L. albiflora ~17–19.5 mm and conspicuously two-lipped; L. sempervirens longer narrow trumpet (~38–48 mm). Fused perfoliate terminal bracts occur in BOTH native species. Mature blackish berries support L. japonica; red berries do NOT separate the natives. Flower color, scent, habit and leaf fusion alone are unreliable.

Diagnostic photo priorities (not acquired): matched whole reproductive shoots with node arrows; L. albiflora vs L. sempervirens side-view corolla with mm scale; fused-bract macros on both; L. japonica axillary cymes and glandular-hair macro; voucher-linked ripe fruit comparison.

## LOOK HERE FIRST
1. **Petiole orientation:** P. deltoides petiole flattened at right angle to blade plane; P. trichocarpa (POWO P. tristis) cylindrical or only flattened in blade plane, often swollen near blade.
2. **Leaf margins and undersides:** P. deltoides usually coarser translucent/ciliate teeth; P. trichocarpa finer teeth and often whitish abaxial surface with red resin stains. Blade shapes and juvenile leaves overlap.
3. **Basilaminar glands + winter buds:** FNA subsp. deltoides usually 3–6 tubular glands and glabrous buds; subsp. monilifera usually 2 round glands and hairy buds; subsp. wislizeni usually no glands and hairy buds. All three occur in Texas; intergrades occur, so never assign a subspecies from a single leaf. P. trichocarpa has 0–2 round glands.
4. **Female fruiting catkins:** P. deltoides ovoid glabrous capsules usually 8–11 mm, saucer-shaped disc 1–3 mm; P. trichocarpa spherical often hairy capsules 7–9 mm, cup-shaped disc 4–6 mm. Sizes overlap; combine shape, pubescence, disc.
5. **Insufficient evidence:** one leaf, bark, cottony seeds, or catkin silhouette alone is not a species-level determination.

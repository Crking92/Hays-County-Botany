## Family -> genus -> species: LOOK HERE FIRST
Solanaceae: fused 5-merous corolla, stamens attached to corolla, superior usually bicarpellate ovary, berry or capsule. Neither an inflated husk nor poricidal anthers characterize the whole family.
Physalis: follow the 5-lobed calyx from flower to mature fruit: it enlarges into a veined, inflated, papery enclosure around a berry. The genus has solitary near-axillary flowers. **Exception:** *P. solanacea* has a tiny urn-shaped (urceolate) flower, not the usual broad yellow groundcherry flower.

1. Urn-shaped corolla **2.5–4.5 mm**, flowering pedicel **1.5–3 mm**, mature husk **5-ribbed** -> *P. solanacea*.
2. Other corollas rotate to bell-spreading, mature husk typically 10-ribbed. Annual taproot, mostly simple sparse hairs, usually unspotted corolla **6–10 mm**, pedicel **7–17(–22) mm**, husk **20–40 mm** -> *P. angulata*.
3. Perennial rhizomes and branched dendroid/stellate hairs, five dark corolla spots, corolla **(8–)10–16 mm**, usually yellow anthers **2–5 mm**, husk **15–35(–45) mm** -> *P. cinerascens*.
4. Perennial rhizomes, glabrous or sparse **simple antrorse hairs <=0.5 mm** rather than branched hairs -> *P. longifolia*; confirm with multiple reproductive characters, not leaf width alone.

## Accepted names / synonym and concept cautions
- *P. angulata* L.: accepted POWO/FNA; FNA historical *P. pendula*, *P. lanceifolia* and *P. angulata* var. *pendula* synonyms. Narrow-leaved *P. angulata* can mimic *P. acutifolia* without a flower; compare corolla flare.
- *P. cinerascens* (Dunal) Hitchc.: accepted; *P. viscosa* var. *cinerascens* and *P. pensylvanica* var. *cinerascens* synonyms. **FNA's *P. cinerascens* var. *spathulifolia* maps to accepted *P. spathulifolia* in current POWO**; no variety assigned to the source observation.
- *P. longifolia* Nutt.: accepted; POWO accepts vars. *longifolia*, *subglabrata*, *texana*. Historical *P. texana* is synonym of *P. longifolia* var. *texana*, NOT an extra species. No infraspecific ID from Texas location alone.
- *P. solanacea* (Schltdl.) Axelius: accepted; historical genus *Margaranthus*, basionym *M. solanaceus*, with *M. lemmonii*, *M. purpurascens*, *M. tenuis* as synonyms. Distinctive 5-ribbed husk and very short pedicel, not purple color alone.

**Unreliable/overlapping characters:** leaf shape, grayish cast without a hair macro, corolla color alone, and fruiting-husk length overlap. For local confusers also compare *P. mollis* (branched hairs), *P. virginiana* (longer divergent/retrorse hairs), *P. acutifolia* (strongly flaring corolla), *Chamaesaracha* (calyx not inflated to enclose berry) and *Solanum* (typically exposed berry).

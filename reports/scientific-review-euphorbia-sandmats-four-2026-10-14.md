**Key caution:** common word “golondrina” is applied to multiple Euphorbia species; it is not a taxon ID. Do not confuse *E. golondrina* with *E. ophthalmica* or *E. hirta* because of a vernacular name.

## Family → genus: WHY Euphorbiaceae and Euphorbia?
Euphorbiaceae is diverse; **milky latex and a cyathium are characteristic of many Euphorbia, not universal family characters**. For these four species, look for opposite asymmetrical leaves and stipules, a **cyathium** (cup-shaped involucre containing separate tiny male flowers and one stalked female flower), rim glands and appendages, and a usually 3-part capsule. Do not label cyathial appendages 'petals' or the entire cyathium 'one bisexual flower'. Check actual structures at magnification.

## LOOK HERE FIRST — four-way morphological key (FNA species descriptions)
1. **Habit and arrangement of cyathia**. Erect/ascending, mostly glabrous 15–50 cm plant with dense terminal **and axillary** clusters suggests *E. hypericifolia*. Prostrate plants need further inspection.
2. **Prostrate and glabrous?** *E. golondrina* has glabrous stems/leaves and **solitary distal-node cyathia** on peduncles about 0.9–1.5 mm; comparatively large, deeply concave red/purple glands (~0.3–0.4 mm). Its mature capsule ~1.7–2 mm long, glabrous; seed ~1.3–1.5 mm with faint ridges. **Do not confuse** with the canonical *E. serpens* glabrous mat; verify node rooting, stipules, gland macro and seed.
3. **Prostrate with dense terminal heads and mixed short/long hairs?** *E. ophthalmica*: stems often strigillose **and** hirsute; cyathia clustered in dense terminal glomerules, often with reduced bractlike leaves; involucre 0.5–0.7 mm long; capsule ~1–1.2 mm and strigillose; seed ~0.7–0.9(–1.1) mm with faint transverse ridges. A reddish leaf blotch may occur and is **not** proof.
4. **Prostrate mat with evenly hairy stems and capsules?** *E. maculata*: stems densely and evenly short-sericeous/villous, **not rooting at nodes**; leaf underside often woolly/hairy, strong asymmetric base; cyathia solitary or small axillary clusters; capsule ~1.3–1.5 mm, evenly sericeous; seed ~1–1.2 mm with 3–4 low ridges. Red leaf spot is optional and shared with others.
5. **Confirm ambiguous material by mature fruit, seed and stipules**; overall prostrate habit, white latex, leaf blotch and small white gland appendages overlap many sandmats, including canonical *E. prostrata*, *E. serpens* and *E. nutans*. Do not identify from a generic top-down photograph.

**Numerical cautions:** dimensions are published FNA descriptions, not absolute exclusion boundaries; seed/capsule sizes overlap (especially *maculata* vs *ophthalmica*), and hairs may be hard to see on worn or immature specimens. Inspect several nodes and ripe fruits, with scale.

## Exact diagnostic PHOTO ROLES (handoff; not installed or verified)
**Family level:** dissected Euphorbiaceae capsule/three carpels as representative (not universal); representative genus Euphorbia cyathium **clearly labeled subgroup-only**, contrasting an ordinary flower from a different family genus.
**Genus level:** true cyathium macro (rim glands, gland appendages, separate male stamens, central female stalked ovary); opposite asymmetrical leaves and stipules at a node; mature 3-part capsule and seed.
**Species level:**
- *E. golondrina*: prostrate **glabrous** stem/leaf; **single distal-node cyathium** and its stalk; red-purple concave gland macro; ripe glabrous capsule and seed under millimeter scale; matched *E. serpens* rooting-node contrast.
- *E. hypericifolia*: entire **erect/ascending** plant; **axillary plus terminal dense heads**; hairless stem and ovary; larger leaf with marginal teeth; capsule and seed macro; matched *E. ophthalmica* cluster comparison.
- *E. maculata*: evenly hairy stem and **leaf underside**, nonrooting node; hairy capsule, ripe seed ridges; compare with canonical *E. prostrata* (fruit keels vs more even hair distribution) and *E. serpens* (glabrous capsule).
- *E. ophthalmica*: **dense terminal head** with reduced bracts, mixed long/short stem hairs; small hairy capsule; small seed and gland appendage macro; compare with *E. maculata* same magnification.

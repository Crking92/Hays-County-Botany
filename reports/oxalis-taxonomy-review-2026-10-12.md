## LOOK HERE FIRST
**1. Leaf origin:** basal-only trifoliolate leaves from a bulb and separate leafless flowering scapes = drummondii/violacea; leafy aerial stems = yellow caulescent group.

**2. Bulbous pair:** O. drummondii leaflet (6–)14–34 mm, lobed 1/4–4/5, no oxalate lines near notch; O. violacea (5–)8–15(–20) mm, lobed 1/4–1/3, oxalate lines along notch margin. Sizes overlap; photograph notches at same scale and sepal-tip orange tubercles. Flower color insufficient.

**3. Yellow group:** O. dillenii straight forward-appressed nonseptate hairs, 1–3(–8) flowers, petals (2.5–)4–8 mm; O. albicans short curved/crisped forward hairs, usually 0.2–0.3 mm, 1–2(–3) flowers, petals 6–10(–12) mm; O. stricta spreading septate and nonseptate hairs (especially nodes), often 5–7 flowers, petals (6–)8–11 mm. Counts and petal sizes overlap: use hair microscopy plus mature capsules.

**4. Local look-alikes:** O. corniculata has free broad stipule flanges and creeping rooting nodes; O. florida glabrous sepals and sparse curved hairs; O. texana prominent red petal-base lines; O. pilosa Texas reports may be misidentified O. albicans.

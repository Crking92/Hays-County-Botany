# Sesbania pod morphology — scientific correction

Cornell and Missouri MDC describe Sesbania herbacea pods as four-sided; UT Austin and USGS Louisiana describe them as approximately cylindrical or round. Do not teach circular-versus-four-sided cross-section as an absolute species key. Sesbania drummondii has four conspicuous papery pod wings, whereas S. herbacea has a long, narrow pod without those broad wings. Retain all existing learner approvals and unresolved county-range status. Do not install taxa or images. Photo-polish handoff: matched mature attached pods with scale and verified wing edges; annotation pending visual inspection.

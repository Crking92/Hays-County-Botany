## LOOK HERE FIRST
Inspect **the complete flowering branch**: is the coiled, one-sided cyme clustered with other cymes, or more solitary? Then compare a measured flower front and side, stem hairs under magnification, calyx, terminal style, mature capsule and seeds. Flower color, leaf divisions, and a coiled axis alone cannot resolve species.

- **Phacelia congesta Hook.** (POWO accepted): UT Austin describes clustered cymes typically in groups of 3–5, flowers about 6–7 mm across, gland-tipped and stiff hairs, five exserted stamens, bifid terminal style, and a 2-valved capsule. *P. congesta* var. *dissecta* / *P. dissecta* are synonyms, not duplicate taxa.
- **Phacelia patuliflora (Engelm. & A.Gray) A.Gray** (POWO/USDA/ITIS accepted): often a lower, spreading plant with simpler flowering axes and showier flowers, but these characters need matched voucher confirmation. Critically, *P. patuliflora* var. *austrotexana* maps to **accepted P. austrotexana** in POWO. Do not silently merge the segregate or assume it from county alone.
- **Phacelia strictiflora (Engelm. & A.Gray) E.Hall** (POWO accepted): four accepted varieties. ITIS uses a different author citation. Species-level photographic characters remain insufficiently confirmed; do not invent precise measurements or assign a variety from a general photo.

**Family caveat:** project follows broad Boraginaceae, including former Hydrophyllaceae. Phacelia's terminal style and capsule are hydrophylloid clues; classic borage four-nutlet fruits are NOT a universal family character.

## Diagnostic photo handoff
1. Full flowering shoot including the junction of cyme branches.
2. Stem and pedicel glandular hairs at macro scale, plus plain-language hair terminology.
3. Corolla front/side and calyx with millimeter ruler; stamens and terminal style clearly visible.
4. Opened mature capsule and seeds; distinguish capsules from classic borage nutlets.
5. Matched comparison images of *P. congesta* and *P. patuliflora* branch architecture; voucher-backed *P. strictiflora* needed.

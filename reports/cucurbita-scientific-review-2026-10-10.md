# Cucurbita scientific review — 2026-10-10

Science-only handoff; no canonical installation or READY markers.

Current POWO and Flora of North America both recognize Cucurbita melopepo and C. pepo as separate accepted taxa. Historical C. pepo var. melopepo maps to C. melopepo. Texas wild C. melopepo var. texana does not make a source observation of cultivated pattypan squash a confirmed wild native plant.

Look first at perennial tuberous root plus long triangular shallowly lobed leaves for C. foetidissima. For annual C. pepo vs C. melopepo, examine pustulate-based stem hairs and the mature five-ribbed fruit stalk, but these characters overlap. Fruit shape/color alone are unreliable in domesticated squash.

Photo roles: stem hair macro, mature fruit peduncle junction, mature leaf ruler, reproductive flower side view, whole plant/root habit; preserve exact provenance.

Cucumis melo is introduced in Texas. C. pepo is treated as introduced in Texas by POWO. C. foetidissima is Texas native at state level but project three-county proof remains unresolved.

Sources: Flora of North America Cucurbita genus key (taxon 108644); POWO Cucurbita melopepo, C. pepo, C. foetidissima and Cucumis melo (2026).

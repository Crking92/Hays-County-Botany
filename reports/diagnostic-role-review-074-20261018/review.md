1. For *Aristida purpurea*, image 1's awns are clearly visible against a fingertip, but **purple color or three awns is not a species-level ID**. Captions no longer imply a calibrated glume measurement.
2. For *A. oligantha*, image 3 is a clear stem junction, not a glume close-up. Images 4 and 5 show awns and wiry flowering stems without clear venation or proof of annual rootstock.
3. The five locally stored *H. annuus* views distinguish a fresh yellow ray/disk flower from maturing dark heads and a wider plant. Its last two gallery entries are external direct URLs; no unsupported pixel assertion was added.
4. *Bidens* fruit photo 1 shows many dark narrow mature cypselae among pale chaff, but **individual awn length and fruit wing/margin** cannot be diagnosed from this picture. Its third yellow-head URL is external/uninspected in this pass.
5. All captions are photographic visible-feature cues; they **do not certify exact source botanical IDs, diagnostic-scale morphological measurements, cultivar status, or geographical nativity**.

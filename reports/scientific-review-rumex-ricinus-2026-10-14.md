## Rumex LOOK HERE FIRST
Find **mature fruiting valves** (the enlarged three inner tepals), not merely leaf outlines. *R. crispus* generally has entire or nearly entire valve margins; *R. pulcher* typically has conspicuously toothed margins. Photograph multiple ripe fruits, their tubercles, and the **ocrea** (papery sheath at stem nodes). Leaf form overlaps; compare the existing *R. chrysocarpos* native concept using fruits before naming.

## Ricinus family vs genus/species caveat
*Ricinus communis* has palmately lobed leaves and prickly, three-parted capsules; male and female flowers occupy distinct positions in the inflorescence. These features illustrate *Ricinus*, **not universal Euphorbiaceae anatomy**. Capture male/female flowers, complete inflorescence, leaf base/petiole, capsule and opened fruit.

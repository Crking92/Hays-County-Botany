## LOOK HERE FIRST — Datura
1. On a fully opened corolla, count actual apical teeth (not folds). **D. innoxia:** typically five prominent + five smaller secondary teeth; **D. wrightii:** usually five conspicuous teeth.
2. Measure flower length in side view with a scale: *innoxia* commonly 16–20 cm, *wrightii* commonly 20–24 cm; **lengths overlap**.
3. Inspect stem/pedicel hairs at 10x: often more spreading in *innoxia* and more appressed in *wrightii*, but not definitive.
4. Verify calyx and mature capsule posture; compare *D. stramonium* (shorter corolla, often erect capsule) where appropriate.
5. Do not rely on white/purple tint, fragrance, spiny fruit, or habit alone. Older **Datura meteloides** usage can be misapplied: POWO lists *D. meteloides* DC. ex Dunal as synonym of *D. innoxia*, not *D. wrightii*. Both are separate accepted species in POWO.
**Diagnostic photo roles:** matched front-on fully expanded flower mouths with teeth labeled; side view with ruler; stem hair macros; calyx; mature fruit. Source media and reuse rights not locked. All Datura are poisonous; no tasting.

## LOOK HERE FIRST — Evax -> Diaperia
*Evax prolifera* maps to *Diaperia prolifera*: mature heads elongated, ca. 3.5–4.5 mm tall, about 2–3 times as tall as wide, with capitular leaves visible between heads.
*Evax verna* maps to *Diaperia verna*: heads more rounded, ca. 2–3.3 mm, about as tall as wide, with capitular leaves largely hidden.
Woolliness and head counts overlap; varieties intergrade. Reuse the accepted *Diaperia* learner concepts and their planned mature head/branch/palea/cypsela comparison photos. **No alias gallery or duplicate species entry.**

## Accepted taxonomy and status
- Nicotiana glauca Graham: accepted; South American native, introduced in Texas. Learner role NON_NATIVE_COMPARISON; no native metric credit.
- Nicotiana obtusifolia M.Martens & Galeotti: accepted; Texas native at state level. Nicotiana trigonophylla is a synonym. Learner approved_pending_curation; target-zone 3-county status UNRESOLVED.
- Nicotiana repanda Willd.: accepted; Texas native at state level. Nicotiana lyrata and Nicotiana roemeriana are synonyms. Learner approved_pending_curation; target-zone 3-county status UNRESOLVED.

## LOOK HERE FIRST
1. Inspect whole growth form and older stems: glauca is a woody shrub/small tree; obtusifolia can be woody-based, repanda generally annual. Habit supports but does not prove species.
2. Inspect several upper leaf bases and surfaces: glauca usually smooth and glaucous with petioles; obtusifolia often glandular/viscid, with some clasping upper leaves; repanda often fiddle-shaped and clasping. Upper leaf clasping OVERLAPS obtusifolia and repanda.
3. Compare the FULL corolla tube in profile with scale and inspect calyx lobes/ribs. Earlier review reports longer repanda tubes than obtusifolia; ranges may overlap, so do not apply an unverified hard cutoff.
4. Check capsule inside persistent calyx and mature leaves on the same plant. Flower color and one leaf are unreliable.

## Family/genus teaching
Solanaceae: usually fused five-lobed corolla, five epipetalous stamens, superior bicarpellate ovary; exceptions exist. Nicotiana: tubular/funnel-shaped corolla, persistent five-lobed calyx, alternate simple leaves, dry many-seeded capsule. Contrast Physalis berry in inflated calyx, Solanum berry and often poricidal anthers, Datura large trumpet corolla and capsule; these are genus tendencies, not universal family traits.

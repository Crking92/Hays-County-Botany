# Erodium and Eustoma taxonomy handoff (2026-10-11)

Scientific review only. Five Master Plant Pool rows updated and verified via _Master Pool Overrides rows 694-698. No canonical installation or gallery backfill.

## Erodium
- E. cicutarium: accepted, introduced in Texas, non-native comparison. Mature leaf pinnately compound with deeply cut separate leaflets; Geranium cicutarium is a synonym.
- E. texanum: accepted, Texas native at state level; project county threshold unresolved. Mature leaf simple, broadly 3-5 lobed. Flora of North Central Texas gives petals about 6-8 mm versus >=10 mm respectively; confirm by full leaf, not flower hue.
- Both share five fertile stamens plus five staminodes and a five-part long-beaked fruit. These confirm genus, not species. Existing Erodium genus.json already teaches the leaf comparison and should be preserved.
- Joey E. texanum video frame is tagged "dissected leaves"; verify leaf architecture and original provenance before accepting identification.

## Eustoma
- Kew POWO accepts E. exaltatum and E. russellianum separately.
- E. exaltatum subsp. russellianum is a homotypic synonym of accepted E. russellianum, not a third card. NPSOT uses the older subspecies treatment; retain as alias.
- Both accepted species are native Texas at state level; strict project >=3 target counties unresolved.
- Do not force separation from purple flower color, flower size, cultivar status or habitat. Obtain voucher-confirmed matched calyx, corolla tube/throat, opposite clasping leaves, mature fruit, seeds and locality.
- UT Austin's E. russellianum description includes 55-65 mm flower diameter, 16-25 mm calyx and ca. 20 x 8.5-9 mm fruit; not interspecific thresholds.

Sources: Kew Plants of the World Online, Flora of North Central Texas Erodium key, UT Austin Plant Resources Center Eustoma russellianum treatment, Native Plant Society of Texas.

# Veronica scientific review 2026-10-12

Three accepted species reviewed: Veronica anagallis-aquatica, V. persica, V. polita. All are introduced in Texas (POWO) and remain approved non-native learner comparisons. Diagnostic split: fruiting pedicels V. persica 15–40 mm vs V. polita 6–15 mm; measure capsule notch, persistent style and flower width. For V. anagallis-aquatica compare V. catenata using mature racemes and capsules. Measurements overlap; blue flowers alone are insufficient. No canonical installation or photos.

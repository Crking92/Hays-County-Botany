# Chaetopappa diagnostic review — 2026-10-10

Scientific review only; no canonical installation or native-range PASS.

LOOK HERE FIRST: C. ericoides has tightly overlapping leathery leaves with two upper grooves and 20–30 pappus bristles. C. effusa is rhizomatous with flat proximal leaves 6–12 mm wide and a tiny ringlike pappus. C. bellidifolia is an annual with taproot and a minute pappus ring. The ring overlaps effusa and bellidifolia: confirm crown and leaves.

Compare C. parryi (spatulate leaves 2–5 mm; pappus crown 0.3–0.8 mm), C. imberbis (cuplike pappus), C. hersheyi (two pappus series), and distinct C. bellioides. Flower color is not decisive.

# Ligustrum scientific review — 2026-10-12

Scope: scientific review only. No canonical installation or READY markers. All three Master Plant Pool taxa are source-pool learner-approved and nonnative comparisons.

| Species | Accepted taxonomy | Primary distinguishing structures |
|---|---|---|
| *Ligustrum lucidum* W.T.Aiton | accepted, Asian origin | glossy leathery blades often 65–145 mm long; glabrous stems; broad terminal panicle 100–180 mm |
| *Ligustrum quihoui* Carrière | accepted, Asian origin | blades 10–36 × 4–11 mm, narrowed to short petiole; essentially glabrous blade, short-tomentose stems; paired panicle branches |
| *Ligustrum sinense* Lour. | accepted, Asian origin; Texas invasive per NPSOT | blades 13–63 × 10–23 mm, short hairs along lower midrib; short-hairy twigs; leafy axillary/terminal panicles |

LOOK HERE FIRST: measure 5–10 mature leaves and petioles; inspect underside midrib hairs at 10×; check panicle branching and flower placement. Flower color, fruit color, and opposite leaves are shared and unreliable. Both small-leaved taxa have hairy stems. Regional pedicel lengths overlap (*quihoui* up to 2.5 mm; *sinense* 0.8–1.3 mm at anthesis), so stalk presence is not a hard separator. Retain *Ligustrum sp.* for insufficient evidence. Compare *L. japonicum*, *L. vulgare*, and native *Forestiera* (often apetalous).

Taxonomy caution: *L. quihoui* var. *glabrum* is a synonym of *L. gracile*, not *L. quihoui*. *L. brachystachyum* is a synonym of *L. quihoui*. Never infer county-native PASS from source-pool admission.

## Three source concepts
- *Parthenocissus heptaphylla*: recognized by POWO and FNA; typically seven leaflets, 3–5 tendril branches, no adhesive discs; inflorescences repeatedly forked.
- *P. quinquefolia*: recognized by both; typically five leaflets, tendrils often 4–12-branched with adhesive discs, inflorescence with a distinct central axis.
- **Thicket-creeper naming conflict:** POWO accepts *P. inserta* with *P. vitacea* as synonym, while Flora of North America applies *P. vitacea* to the usually disc-less thicket-creeper concept and interprets the name *P. inserta* under *P. quinquefolia*. **Do not auto-merge or install a separate canonical card without concept-level voucher/source reconciliation.** Preserve searchable aliases and learner approval.

## LOOK HERE FIRST
1. Trace a **mature tendril** to its tip: adhesive discs present or absent, number of branches. Immature tendrils can mislead.
2. Examine a complete **flower cluster**: repeated forks vs a distinct central axis.
3. Count leaflets on multiple fully developed leaves (five vs seven is useful but not absolute).
4. Check a mature berry and herbarium voucher if ambiguous; berry diameters overlap.

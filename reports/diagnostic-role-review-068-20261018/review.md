## Important provenance and visual-interpretation safeguards

1. **No anatomical certification from caption labels.** Image descriptions were based on the actual saved contact-sheet pixels and broad botanical organ recognition. Fine hairs, pustular bases, precise size differences, flower sex, and true source taxon determinations require specialist/full-resolution review. A text 'Confirm clue' identifies promising structures, not an expert-confirmed ID.
2. **Reuse restrictions remain unchanged:** the two leaf-hair/fruit/seed macro images of *Euphorbia dentata* and the two macro images of *E. davidii*, attributed to S. R. Turner / Missouri Plants, explicitly say **'copyrighted / public reuse not granted'**. These are private-study references; this audit does not permit publishing or redistributing the photos, and the macro captions now clearly warn about their status.
3. **Joey Santore's two Acalypha photos** are distinct photo identifiers but look nearly identical in the comparison sheet. The second is now labeled as a near-repeat, **not as a second independently useful diagnostic perspective**. Both retain their original CC BY-NC 4.0 attribution and public-use cautions.
4. *Euphorbia bicolor* broad narrow-white-bract views show why the white parts are bracts, but do **not** resolve microscopic floral glands well enough to certify individual cyathia.
5. The four revised species files were read back from GitHub. **15/15 photo roles** have meaningful photo-specific descriptions. **15/15 image records** retain photographer, license, and source URL.

LOOK HERE FIRST: Check mature leaf margins (F. crispum typically crispate; F. cynanchoides usually plane), then dissect flower corona lobes (medial constriction in F. crispum; absent in F. cynanchoides). FNA notes crispum may rarely have plane margins. Mature follicles 10–13 cm and seeds 8–9 × 3–4 mm in crispum versus 5–9 cm and 4–6 × 2–3 mm in the plane-margined cynanchoides group. Inner corolla pubescence overlaps; flower color, geography, and overall twining habit are not definitive. Look-alikes: F. heterophyllum (usually narrower leaves, overlapping widths), F. torreyi (more hirsute), other milkvines.

Synonyms: F. crispum = Sarcostemma crispum, Philibertia crispa; F. cynanchoides = Sarcostemma cynanchoides, Philibertia cynanchoides. Source spelling cynanchodies is not a scientific synonym.

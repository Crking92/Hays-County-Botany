## LOOK HERE FIRST
1. **Ligule at leaf collar:** *Echinochloa crus-galli* lacks a ligule; do not use awn length alone to name it.
2. **Seedhead architecture:** *D. ciliaris* has fingerlike racemes with paired, overlapping spikelets on a winged rachis; *D. cognata* has a more open branching panicle, often with hairy branch bases and mostly solitary spikelets at branchlet tips.
3. **Spikelet dissection:** *Eleusine indica* spikelets have multiple florets; Digitaria spikelets are not equivalent merely because the whole seedhead looks fingerlike. Distinguish one fertile floret from one floret total.
4. **Measurement caution:** earlier reviews reported overlapping *D. ciliaris* (about 2.5–3.3 mm) and *D. cognata* (about 2.2–3.1 mm) spikelets. Do not use size alone.

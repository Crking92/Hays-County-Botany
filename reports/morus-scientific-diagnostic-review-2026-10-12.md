# Morus scientific diagnostic review — 2026-10-12

Scientific review of three source-pool names; no canonical installation.

- **Morus alba L.**: accepted; introduced in Texas, learner NON_NATIVE_COMPARISON.
- **Morus microphylla Buckley**: accepted; Texas native at state level; three-target-county certification UNRESOLVED. POWO synonyms M. confinis, M. grisea, M. radulina.
- **Morus rubra L.**: accepted; Texas native at state level; target-county certification UNRESOLVED.

LOOK HERE FIRST: examine mature leaf undersides at 10× for hairs between veins versus vein-only, then upper-surface roughness, mature leaf size, bud scales, and female flower styles. M. rubra usually has interveinal soft hairs; M. alba generally lacks these. Published M. microphylla accounts differ in lower-surface hair distribution, so do not treat a single hair pattern as conclusive. M. alba × M. rubra hybrids may be intermediate. Leaf lobing, fruit color and plant height are unreliable.

## Important safeguards

**Visible organ does not equal an independently verified botanical species identification.** A photo caption is a **training cue**, not an expert determination. Particularly, the *Iva annua* first photo contains fairly ragweed-like green clustered spikes; its species-source match needs independent review before any diagnostic arrow or "confirmed ID" badge. The leafy photo contains small white flowers belonging to background clover-like vegetation, not proof of Iva flower morphology.

All *Ambrosia psilostachya* photos 2–4 originate from the **same iNaturalist observation**. They are three views, not three independent species identifications. Large repository JPEGs were inspected using the verified contact sheet because their original >1 MB payloads did not return through the file viewer; no arbitrary claims about unseen microscopic details were made.

The Acourtia Joey pictures remain CC BY-NC and provisional at source level. The Eclipta and other media retain their original licenses and attribution. No rights were extended by these caption changes.

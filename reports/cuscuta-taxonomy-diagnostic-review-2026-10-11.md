**LOOK HERE FIRST sequence:** (1) dissect style union; (2) examine ripe capsule apex and stylopodium; (3) compare corolla lobes to tube with scale; (4) corroborate with calyx, fringed infrastaminal scales and fruit. C. campestris, C. runyonii and other dodders are realistic look-alikes. South Texas regional key is not a comprehensive modern FNA key. Do not claim a definitive species identification from a single overlapping character.

**Diagnostic photo roles:** exact-species host+vine context; mature flower front/side; dissected style and inner corolla scales; ripe fruit summit and seed with mm ruler; paired C. obtusiflora / C. pentagona lobe-ratio comparison.
